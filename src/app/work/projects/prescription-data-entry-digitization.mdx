## Overview

Led end-to-end product development for a prescription data entry and fax digitization initiative across Loblaws' network of 1,400+ pharmacy locations. The project aimed to modernize outdated manual processes, reduce pharmacist administrative burden, and improve patient care efficiency.

## The Problem

Pharmacists were spending excessive time on manual prescription data entry and fax-based communication, creating:
- Increased risk of transcription errors
- Slower prescription processing times
- Lower pharmacist satisfaction
- Suboptimal patient experience

## The Solution

Designed and executed a comprehensive product roadmap to digitize the prescription workflow:

- **Digital Fax Integration**: Built seamless fax-to-digital conversion pipeline
- **Streamlined Data Entry**: Created intuitive prescription input interface
- **Workflow Queue System**: Implemented prioritized task management
- **Enhanced Compliance**: Built HIPAA/PHIPA compliant data handling

## My Role

As Product Owner/Product Manager, I:
- Owned the complete product lifecycle from discovery to delivery
- Managed 80+ User Stories in the product backlog
- Led cross-functional teams including regulatory SMEs, QA, and developers
- Conducted user research with 20 pharmacy stores nationwide
- Oversaw agile ceremonies (sprint planning, grooming)
- Managed release schedules and communicated with senior leadership

## Key Features

- **Automated Fax Processing**: AI-powered document processing reducing manual entry by 50%
- **User-Centric Interface**: Designed through customer journey mapping and pharmacist feedback
- **Real-time Workflow Queue**: Prioritized task management improving efficiency
- **Compliance-First Design**: 99.9% HIPAA/PHIPA compliance with zero security breaches

## Challenges

- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Coordinating across multiple business units and technical teams
- **Regulatory Requirements**: Navigating complex healthcare compliance landscape
- **Change Management**: Rolling out new workflows to 1,400+ locations
- **Technical Dependencies**: Managing integration between legacy and new systems

## Impact

The transformation delivered measurable business outcomes:

- **30% improvement** in user satisfaction among pharmacists
- **50% faster** prescription processing times through AI automation
- **95% on-time delivery** across all product releases
- **99.9% compliance rate** with zero security breaches
- **95% user acceptance rate** during UAT with 60% reduction in post-launch defects
- **25% faster task completion** for pharmacy staff

## Learnings

This project reinforced the importance of:
- Deep user research before solution design
- Balancing regulatory constraints with user experience
- Effective cross-functional collaboration
- Continuous feedback loops with end-users

The success of this initiative established a blueprint for future digital transformation projects within the pharmacy organization.
